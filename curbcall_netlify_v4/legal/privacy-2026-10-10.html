Short version: we collect the minimum we need to run the Service — your email for your account, what you search for, your subscription status, and the company and bid details you choose to save so the app can fill in paperwork for you. Concrete prices you share are only ever shown pooled with other contractors' and never with your name. We don't sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how CurbCall Pro (the "Service"), operated by Oblique Systems, LLC, handles your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account info: your email address and basic profile when you sign up, including via Google sign-in.
  • What you enter: the ZIP codes, cities, and search radius you use to find bids, and the bids you save.
  • A device identifier: a random ID stored in your browser to manage trials and unlock access.
  • Your location, and only if you ask for it: tapping Use My Location sends your device's coordinates to a geolocation service to work out which town you are in. We keep the town, not a continuing record of where you go, and nothing is sent unless you tap it.
  • Payment info: handled entirely by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your subscription and your billing email — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Company bid details, if you add them: your street address, title, license or registration numbers, years in business, insurance carrier and agent, bonding company, agent and agent's email, and ready-mix supplier and supplier's email. We use them only to fill in your bid forms and write your bond and concrete requests. We never ask for a tax ID, a signature or anything that has to be sworn.
  • Phone alerts, if you turn them on: the address and keys your browser gives us for sending a notification to that device. When a device stops accepting alerts we delete its entry, and the rest go when you delete your account.
  • Concrete quotes you save: the price per yard, yards, delivery and short-load fees and mix strength, kept with the bid you saved them on. If you choose to add a quote to local prices, it is stored with your account and the job's location rounded to about 7 miles, never the exact address.
  • If you leave a review: the rating, quote, display name and company you enter. Reviews are shown publicly on our website once we approve them, so treat what you write there as public.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service — run your searches and show you bids.
  • To watch the bids you save. About every six hours we re-read each saved, still-open bid posting for addenda and new documents, and the day before a bid is due we remind you, by phone alert if you turned those on, otherwise by email.
  • To show local concrete prices. A price range for an area appears only once at least three quotes from at least two different contractors are on file there. Quotes are never shown one at a time or with a name.
  • To fill in bid forms. Our server writes your details into the agency's form. To work out which box is which, the form's box labels go to OpenAI; your details do not.
  • To manage your account, trial, and subscription.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To improve and support the Service.

3. Service providers we share with

We use trusted third parties to run the Service, and share only what's needed:

  • Supabase — accounts and sign-in (including Google login), saved bids, and your company profile.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • OpenAI — reads the text of public bid postings and bid documents to pull out the details, and matches the boxes on a bid form to the kind of information each one wants. It is not sent your company details.
  • Tavily, Brave Search and DuckDuckGo — web searches run on your behalf. These receive the place you are searching for, and Brave also the bid's town when we look for ready-mix plants near it.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass) — the public map we search for ready-mix plants near a bid. It receives the area around the bid's town, nothing about you.
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla and Microsoft push services — deliver phone alerts to your device, only if you turn them on. Each alert is encrypted so only your device can read it.
  • Zippopotam, Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) and BigDataCloud — turn a ZIP, a place name, or your device's location into coordinates so we can measure distance. BigDataCloud is used only if you tap Use My Location.
  • SAM.gov — federal contract opportunities.
  • Resend — sends the email we send you, such as bid alerts and account mail.
  • Upstash — durable storage for scan results and account records.
  • Render and Cloudflare — hosting for the backend and the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information about other people

Two parts of the Service handle information about people who are not our customers. Both come from sources that are already public, and we describe them here because you should know what the Service does, not only what it does with your own data.

  • Contacts on a bid. A bid posting usually names a contracting officer or purchasing agent, sometimes with a phone number or email. We show that alongside the bid so you can reach them — it is published by the agency for exactly that purpose. We show it per bid; we do not build or sell contact lists from it.
  • Plan holders. Some agencies publish a list of the contractors who have taken plans for a job. Where that list is public we show it, because knowing who else is bidding is useful.

When you ask the app to write a quote to a prime contractor, or a request to your bonding agent or concrete supplier, it opens in your own email app. You see it and send it yourself; we don't send it and don't keep a copy.

We also keep a list of construction businesses we contact about CurbCall Pro, built from public business listings. Every message tells you how to opt out, and an opt-out is honoured immediately and permanently. If you would rather not be contacted at all, email support@curbcallpro.com and we will remove you.

5. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your saved bids and device ID. It does not work without this basic storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics. Our hosting and security providers may set a cookie of their own to keep the site available and to tell real visitors from automated traffic.

6. Data retention

We keep account and subscription data while your account is active. Billing and tax records are kept for seven years after the transaction, which is the period tax authorities can ask about them. You can ask us to delete your account data at any time (see below).

One record is kept after deletion: the fact that your account accepted these Terms and this Privacy Policy, which versions, when, and the email address that accepted them. We keep it to evidence that agreement if a dispute arises later, which is a purpose the law recognises. We keep it for ten years after you delete your account — the longest period in which a claim about a written agreement could still be brought in the states where we operate — and then delete it. Everything else — your saved bids, notes, pipeline, company profile and bid details, saved searches, concrete quotes, phone alert subscriptions and account itself — is deleted when you ask.

7. Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You can cancel your subscription anytime and sign out from the Account screen.

8. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, this section supplements the rest of this policy and summarizes your rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

  • What we collect, in CCPA's categories: identifiers (your email address); commercial information (your subscription and billing status, and any concrete quotes you save); internet or network activity (the searches you run, bids you save, and basic technical logs); and, only if you tap Use My Location, approximate geolocation.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising — we don't run advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no sale or share to opt out of.
  • Your rights: to know what we've collected about you, to request deletion, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights. These are the same rights described in Section 7, restated here because California law asks us to name them specifically.
  • How to exercise them: email support@curbcallpro.com from the address on your account, or have an authorized agent contact us with your written permission. We verify a request against the account before acting on it, acknowledge it within 10 business days, and respond within 45 calendar days (extendable once, by 45 more days, with notice to you if a request is complex).

9. Children

The Service is for business users and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. How we protect your information

Everything travels over an encrypted connection. Your password is never seen or stored by us — sign-in is handled by Supabase, and card numbers are handled entirely by Stripe. Access to the systems holding customer data is limited to the people who need it, which today is one person. Records that exist to evidence your agreement to these documents cannot be edited after they are written.

No service can promise perfect security, and we do not. What we can promise is that we will not stay quiet: if we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and any regulator we are required to notify, without unreasonable delay and in line with the law of the state you live in.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post changes here with a new "Last updated" date. Every version we publish is kept at Previous versions. When we make a material change, the app asks you to review and accept it the next time you sign in.

12. Contact

Questions or requests? Contact us at support@curbcallpro.com.
