Common questions

How much does concrete sidewalk cost in Illinois?

On IDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for pc concrete sidewalk, 5 in. was $13.93 a sq ft, from 449 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does curb and gutter cost in Illinois?

On IDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for combination curb and gutter, b-6.12 was $56.80 a foot, from 286 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does concrete driveway cost in Illinois?

On IDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for pcc driveway pavement, 6 in. was $139.11 a sq yd, from 159 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much is ready-mix concrete per yard?

Typically $110–$175 a cubic yard in 2026, before any delivery charge, and often less in the Midwest and Southeast. Orders under a full truck (about 10 yards) usually carry a short-load charge. A local plant's quote is the real number.
