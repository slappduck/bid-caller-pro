How much does concrete sidewalk cost in Missouri?

On MoDOT jobs (2025), the average bid for concrete sidewalk, 4 in. was $69.38 a sq yd, from 198 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much is ready-mix concrete per yard?

Typically $110–$175 a cubic yard in 2026, before any delivery charge, and often less in the Midwest and Southeast. Orders under a full truck (about 10 yards) usually carry a short-load charge. A local plant's quote is the real number.
