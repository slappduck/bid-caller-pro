Common questions

How much does concrete sidewalk cost in Indiana?

On INDOT jobs (2025), the average bid for concrete sidewalk was $64.95 a sq yd. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does ada curb ramp cost in Indiana?

On INDOT jobs (2025), the average bid for concrete curb ramp (ada) was $208.47 a sq yd. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does curb and gutter cost in Indiana?

On INDOT jobs (2025), the average bid for concrete curb and gutter was $31.62 a foot. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much is ready-mix concrete per yard?

Typically $110–$175 a cubic yard in 2026, before any delivery charge, and often less in the Midwest and Southeast. Orders under a full truck (about 10 yards) usually carry a short-load charge. A local plant's quote is the real number.
