Common questions

How much does concrete sidewalk cost in Minnesota?

On MnDOT jobs (2025), the average winning price for concrete walk, 4 in. was $8.28 a sq ft, from 21 contracts. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does concrete driveway cost in Minnesota?

How much is ready-mix concrete per yard?

Typically $110–$175 a cubic yard in 2026, before any delivery charge, and often less in the Midwest and Southeast. Orders under a full truck (about 10 yards) usually carry a short-load charge. A local plant's quote is the real number.
