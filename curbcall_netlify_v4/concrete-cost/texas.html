Common questions

How much does concrete sidewalk cost in Texas?

On TxDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for concrete sidewalk, 4 in. was $106.27 a sq yd, from 575 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does ada curb ramp cost in Texas?

On TxDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for curb ramp, type 1 (ada) was $3,114 each, from 465 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does curb and gutter cost in Texas?

On TxDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for curb and gutter, type ii was $57.74 a foot, from 618 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much does concrete driveway cost in Texas?

On TxDOT jobs (2026), the average bid for concrete driveway was $134.90 a sq yd, from 754 bids. That's state highway work; city jobs can run different, and the price includes labor, materials and the contractor's markup.

How much is ready-mix concrete per yard?

Typically $110–$175 a cubic yard in 2026, before any delivery charge, and often less in the Midwest and Southeast. Orders under a full truck (about 10 yards) usually carry a short-load charge. A local plant's quote is the real number.
