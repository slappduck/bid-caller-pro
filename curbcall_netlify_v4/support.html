Billing & cancelling

What does it cost?

Every new account gets a 14-day free trial, no card needed. After that it's $49 a month or $399 a year. Both renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel?
  1. In the app, open Account and tap Manage billing, or use the billing page directly.
  2. Enter the email you paid with. Stripe, our payment company, emails you a sign-in link.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription.

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do you give refunds?

Payments already made aren't refunded, including partial months. If you were charged by mistake or something went wrong with billing, email us and we'll make it right.

My card was declined or a payment failed.

Stripe tries the card again over the next few days and emails you. To fix it right away, open Manage billing, sign in with the email you paid with, and update your card.

How do I switch between monthly and yearly, or get a receipt?

Both are on the billing page: past invoices and receipts, your card and your plan. If you don't see the switch you want, email us and we'll do it for you.

Will I be reminded before a yearly plan renews?

Yes. Yearly plans get an email a few weeks before they renew, with the price and a link to cancel. If a price ever changes, we email at least 30 days before it applies to you.

I paid, but the app is still locked.

The app unlocks on its own when you paid with the same email you signed in with. If you used a different email at checkout, open Account, find Have a license key? and paste the key from your receipt email. Still stuck? Email us with both addresses.

Signing in & your account

How do I use it on a new phone or computer?

Go to curbcallpro.com/app and sign in with the same email or Google account. Your saved bids sync over.

Can I put it on my home screen like an app?

iPhone: open curbcallpro.com/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

How do I change my email or password?

In the app, under Account → Sign-in & security. A new email gets a confirmation link, and your old one keeps working until you click it.

Can I use the app in Spanish?

Sí. Open Account, scroll to Language and choose Español.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, at the bottom of Account, type DELETE and tap Delete my account. It removes your account, saved bids and company info, and cancels any subscription. See the Privacy Policy for what's kept and for how long.

Bids & prices

Where do the bids come from?

From public postings: city, county, school district and state bid pages, plus federal jobs on SAM.gov. CurbCall Pro checks them every day and keeps the concrete work: sidewalk, curb and gutter, ADA ramps, driveways and flatwork.

A bid's details look wrong.

Always check the agency's own posting before you bid. The deadline, scope and addenda there are what count. Tell us about the mistake so we can fix how it's read.

My town isn't showing up.

Email us the town and, if you know it, the page where it posts bids. We'll add it to the scans. Agencies can also post a job for free on the post a bid page.

Where do the target prices come from?

From what the same work went for on public bid results: your state's own lettings where they're published, nearby states where they aren't, and city results as they're posted. Each price says where it came from. They're a guide, not a quote, and you can type your own prices over them.

Alerts

I'm not getting bid alerts on my phone.
  1. In the app, open Account → Bid Alerts and turn them on.
  2. iPhone: alerts only work from the home screen icon, so add it first (see above) and turn alerts on from there.
  3. Check that your phone's settings allow notifications for CurbCall or your browser.
