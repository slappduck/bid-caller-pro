# 24 lines explaining what content signals mean and then declaring none of
# them, and it carries no Sitemap line -- so it granted nothing, restricted
# nothing, and pointed nowhere. This replaces it with the same reservation of
# rights plus actual directives.

# gives nothing back, so it is declined.
