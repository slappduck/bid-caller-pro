For agencies

Post a bid notice

Free, for any city, county, township or district. Post sidewalk, ADA, curb or concrete work and it reaches concrete contractors already searching your area. No account, no cost, nothing to install.

This is not a procurement system and it does not replace your legal notice. It takes no bids and no attachments — it's a listing, so contractors know the work exists and who to call. Most states require legal notices in a newspaper of record; keep doing that. This runs alongside it.
Quantities help a contractor decide fast. Rough numbers are fine.
What happens next. A person reviews every submission before it appears — usually within a day — so nothing goes live automatically. Once approved it shows up for contractors scanning near you. To change or pull a notice, email support@curbcallpro.com.
