Common questions

Where are concrete and sidewalk bids posted in Alabama?

Mostly on each city, county and school district's own website, one page per agency, plus the state's own lettings. CurbCall Pro watches 154 agency bid pages in Alabama and lists the concrete work it finds: sidewalk, curb and gutter, ADA ramps, driveways and flatwork.

How do I bid a city sidewalk job?

Get the bid documents (some agencies have you register as a plan holder first), read every addendum, price each line item, get a bid bond if the posting asks for one, fill out the agency's own bid form, and submit it before the deadline the way the posting says: sealed envelope or online.
