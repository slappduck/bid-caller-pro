FAQ

Common questions

What exactly does CurbCall Pro do?

It scans government and municipal sites near you, reads their bid pages, and uses AI to pull out live sidewalk, ADA ramp, and curb & gutter bids — the title, scope, deadline, and who to contact. Instead of checking dozens of city sites by hand, you get a clean feed of concrete jobs near you.

Do I have to install anything?

No — CurbCall Pro runs right in your browser on phone or desktop. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app on your phone. If you'd rather have a dedicated desktop app on Windows, download it here — same account, same bids.

Do I need to know the city websites?

No. Just enter your ZIP or let the app detect your location, pick a radius, and it finds the sites for you.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days of full access with no credit card required. Find real bids first, then decide. When the trial ends, subscribe to keep going.

How do payments work?

Payments run securely through Stripe. Choose Monthly or Annual and complete checkout — the app unlocks automatically, and you can restore access on any device with your email. Cancel anytime.
